What is CivicFix?

CivicFix is a platform designed to simplify the process of reporting local civic issues. It enables citizens to quickly report problems such as potholes, garbage accumulation, broken streetlights, and water leaks, while allowing them to track the progress of their complaints.

The Problem

Inefficient Complaint Systems

Many civic reporting systems are slow, complicated, and difficult for citizens to access.

Lack of Transparency

Citizens often do not know whether their complaints have been received or resolved.

Unreported Issues

Many infrastructure problems remain unresolved because people don't know where to report them.

Our Solution

Simple Reporting

Users can report civic issues quickly using a simple online form with location and images.

Transparent Tracking

Citizens can track the status of their reports until the issue is resolved.

Community Engagement

The platform encourages community participation in improving local infrastructure.

How CivicFix Works

Create an Account

Sign up to access the civic issue reporting platform.

Submit a Report

Provide details about the issue including description, location and optional image.

Track Resolution

Monitor the status of your complaint until it is resolved.
